The Big Idea

Once upon a time,
play built minds.

That time is now

Toys have come a long way. The average child carries their own smartphone in their pocket. That’s not progress, though. For child development, less is more. Unfettered Internet access, reward-heavy gamification and short-form stimulation wear down a child’s ability to focus, process and think things through.

Yesterplay strikes the middle ground between board games and iPhones. Each toy is built for maximum engagement and maximum development. Pocketable forms, solo and team play, and modern light and sound keep children involved. The retro heart of every game keeps minds firing in all the right ways.

100% Offline

No connection, no WiFi chip, no OTA updates, and no hacking risk.

Endlessly Engaging

Modern features designed to grab the attention of a digital generation.

Built for Growing Minds

Every game trains recall, speed and pattern-spotting, without ever feeling like a lesson.

Durable Electronics

Built tough. Minimal moving pieces. Good old replaceable alkaline batteries.
